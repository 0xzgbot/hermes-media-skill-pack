## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This skill encodes the visual and atmospheric vocabulary of the Italian *giallo* film genre — the stylized horror/thriller movement pioneered by Mario Bava and Dario Argento in the 1970s. It covers the signature color gel combinations (magenta + green), black leather glove close-ups, baroque framing, Goblin synth-prog atmosphere, and the specific tension between lurid beauty and visceral violence that defines the genre.

When this skill is active, the agent should:
- Use giallo-specific color terms (magenta gel, acid green, blood-red luminosity)
- Apply baroque composition and cryptic camera angles
- Reference the black glove / masked killer iconography
- Build scenes using suspense-through-style rather than jump-scares
- Understand the emotional register: dread wrapped in beauty, candy-colored nightmare

## 2. THE GIALLO AESTHETIC PHILOSOPHY

Giallo is not standard horror — it is **horror as high fashion**. [^72^] [^76^]

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description | Prompt Vocabulary |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Beauty as Dread** | The most terrifying scenes are also the most visually beautiful | "beautiful dread", "lurid beauty", "candy-colored nightmare" |
| **Style Over Substance** | Plot is secondary to visual and sonic atmosphere | "atmosphere-driven", "style as narrative", "visual excess" |
| **The Gaze as Weapon** | Camera voyeuristically watches victims; audience is implicated | "voyeuristic gaze", "surveillance framing", "implicated viewer" |
| **Fashion as Victim** | Victims are impeccably dressed; violence destroys beauty | "fashion victim", "impeccable style", "violence against beauty" |
| **Sound as Assault** | Music and sound design are aggressive, propulsive, disorienting | "sonic assault", "propulsive score", "disorienting soundscape" |
| **Color as Emotion** | Color gels create emotional states independent of narrative | "color as emotion", "gel-driven mood", "chromatic psychology" |

## 3. COLOR & LIGHTING: THE GIALLO GEL SYSTEM

Giallo lighting is created with **theatrical color gels** — not naturalistic motivation. [^72^] [^76^]

### Signature Gel Combinations

| Combination | Colors | Emotional Effect | Films |
|-------------|--------|------------------|-------|
| **Magenta + Blue** | #FF00FF + #0066FF | Dreamlike unease, supernatural threat | *Suspiria*, *Inferno* |
| **Red + Green** | #CC0000 + #00AA00 | Christmas horror, poison, unnatural pairing | *Blood and Black Lace* |
| **Deep Red Monochrome** | #8B0000 dominant | Blood as style, passion as danger | *Deep Red* |
| **Amber + Magenta** | #FFBF00 + #FF00FF | Warm domesticity invaded by unnatural color | *Tenebre* |
| **Acid Green + Purple** | #39FF14 + #4B0082 | Toxicity, madness, chemical threat | *Inferno* |
| **White + Blood Red** | #FFFFFF + #CC0000 | Clinical setting invaded by violence | *Tenebre* |

### Lighting Rules

| Rule | Description | Prompt Terms |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Unmotivated Color** | Lights have no logical source — they exist for emotion only | "unmotivated color gel", "theatrical lighting", "emotion-driven illumination" |
| **Hard Shadows** | Sharp, defined shadows create graphic patterns | "hard graphic shadow", "sharp shadow pattern", "chiaroscuro extreme" |
| **Color Bleed** | Gels bleed into skin tones, making humans look alien | "color-bleached skin", "gel-tinted complexion", "unnatural flesh tone" |
| **Luminous Blood** | Blood glows rather than darkens — it is beautiful | "luminous blood", "glowing crimson", "beautiful violence" |
| **Strobing / Pulsing** | Lights flicker or pulse with music/suspense | "strobing light", "pulsing illumination", "rhythmic light flicker" |

## 4. COMPOSITION & CAMERA

Giallo composition is **baroque, operatic, and deliberately artificial**. [^72^]

| Technique | Description | Prompt Terms |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Cryptic Camera Angles** | Dutch angles, extreme high/low angles, fish-eye distortion | "cryptic angle", "dutch tilt", "extreme high angle", "fish-eye distortion" |
| **The Black Glove Close-Up** | Extreme close-up of killer's leather-gloved hands | "black leather glove close-up", "gloved hand detail", "killer's hands" |
| **Voyeuristic Long Shot** | Camera watches from distance as violence occurs | "voyeuristic long shot", "distant surveillance", "helpless wide view" |
| **Baroque Framing** | Ornate mirrors, doorways, staircases frame the action | "baroque framing", "ornate mirror reflection", "architectural frame within frame" |
| **Point-of-View Stalking** | Subjective camera follows victim from killer's perspective | "POV stalking shot", "subjective killer perspective", "predatory camera" |
| **Reflection Distortion** | Mirrors, water, glass distort and fragment the image | "reflection distortion", "fragmented mirror image", "water surface distortion" |
| **Tilted World** | Camera tilt creates sense of world slipping, disequilibrium | "tilted world", "disequilibrium", "slipping reality" |

## 6. SOUND & MUSIC ATMOSPHERE

Giallo sound design is **aggressive and propulsive**:
- **Goblin (band):** Prog-rock synth with driving bass, whispered vocals, dissonant keyboards
- **Whispers:** Voices barely audible, creating paranoia
- **Breathing:** Heavy, amplified breathing during stalking sequences
- **Stingers:** Sudden musical/sound hits at reveal moments
- **Silence as Setup:** Long quiet passages before sonic assault

**Prompt Translation:**
> "Goblin synth-prog atmosphere", "driving bass and dissonant keyboard", "whispered vocal texture", "amplified breathing tension", "sudden stinger hit"
