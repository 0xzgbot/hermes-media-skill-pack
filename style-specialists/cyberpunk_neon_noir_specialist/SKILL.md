## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This skill encodes the complete visual vocabulary of cyberpunk and neon noir — from Ridley Scott's *Blade Runner* (1982) and Denis Villeneuve's *Blade Runner 2049* (2017) to *Akira*, *Ghost in the Shell*, and contemporary synthwave aesthetics. It covers neon lighting physics, volumetric fog as storytelling device, CRT/scanline textures, holographic interfaces, rain-slick urban environments, and the specific color science of magenta-cyan neon contrast.

When this skill is active, the agent should:
- Use cyberpunk-specific lighting terms (neon bloom, volumetric fog, caustic reflection)
- Apply the magenta-cyan complementary contrast system
- Reference specific material properties (wet asphalt, oxidized metal, plexiglass)
- Build scenes using dystopian scale and human insignificance
- Understand the emotional register: isolation, longing, technological anxiety

## 2. THE CYBERPUNK VISUAL PHILOSOPHY

Cyberpunk is not just "neon and rain" — it is a visual thesis about **technology's collision with humanity**. [^55^] [^57^]

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description | Prompt Vocabulary |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **High Tech, Low Life** | Advanced technology coexists with social decay | "high tech low life", "advanced decay", "technological squalor" |
| **Neon as Life** | Artificial light replaces natural sun; neon is the only warmth | "neon as life source", "artificial warmth", "synthetic sun" |
| **Scale Inequality** | Massive corporate structures dwarf human figures | "monumental scale inequality", "human insignificance", "corporate megastructure" |
| **Wet Surfaces** | Rain, condensation, and humidity make light tangible | "wet surface reflection", "rain-slick asphalt", "condensation texture" |
| **Layered Density** | Visual clutter — signs, cables, advertisements, graffiti — creates information overload | "visual density", "information overload", "layered urban clutter" |

## 3. NEON LIGHTING SCIENCE

Neon cinematography follows specific color science and physical rules. [^55^] [^57^]

### The Magenta-Cyan System

The defining cyberpunk color pairing is **magenta (#FF00FF) and cyan (#00FFFF)** — complementary colors that create maximum vibrational tension.

| Color | Hex | Emotional Function | Physical Source |
|-------|-----|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Magenta** | #FF00FF | Danger, desire, nightlife, vice | Neon bar signs, club lighting, holographic ads |
| **Cyan** | #00FFFF | Technology, coldness, medicine, control | Terminal screens, medical bays, police lights |
| **Amber** | #FFBF00 | Warning, warmth in cold world, nostalgia | Street lamps, old CRT glow, cigarette embers |
| **Acid Green** | #39FF14 | Toxicity, biohazard, mutation, corruption | Toxic waste, bio-luminescence, corrupted data |
| **Deep Purple** | #4B0082 | Mystery, corporate power, luxury | Corporate logos, VIP areas, rare technology |
| **Blood Red** | #8B0000 | Violence, systemic failure, raw humanity | Emergency lights, blood, rebellion |

### Neon Lighting Types

| Light Source | Description | Visual Effect | Prompt Terms |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| **Neon Tube** | Glass tube filled with gas (neon = red, argon = blue) | Sharp linear glow with soft halo | "neon tube glow", "linear gas discharge", "sharp neon edge with soft bloom" |
| **LED Panel** | Grid-based light source | Even, clinical, controllable | "LED panel wash", "clinical grid light", "even technological illumination" |
| **Holographic Projection** | Volumetric light display | Translucent, flickering, data-rich | "holographic projection", "volumetric display", "translucent data ghost" |
| **CRT/Scanline** | Cathode ray tube emission | Flickering, curved, phosphor glow | "CRT phosphor glow", "scanline flicker", "curved screen distortion" |
| **Practical Street Light** | Sodium vapor, mercury vapor | Harsh, monochromatic, atmospheric | "sodium vapor streetlight", "harsh monochromatic pool", "practical urban light" |

### Volumetric Fog as Story Device

Fog in cyberpunk is not atmospheric — it is **narrative**. [^55^]
- **Outdoor fog:** Toxic air, environmental collapse, obscured vision = danger
- **Indoor haze:** Smoke, steam, condensation = lived-in decay
- **Fog as guide:** Key elements unobstructed by fog; background lost in haze = depth control
- **Color diffusion:** Fog scatters neon light, making color tangible in air

**Prompt Translation:**
> "volumetric fog as storytelling device, toxic air obscuring distance, neon light scattering through particulate atmosphere, color diffusion in humid air, fog-guided composition"

| **Holographic Surface** | Iridescent, shifting, data-patterned | Rainbow sheen, pixelated texture, glitch | "holographic iridescence", "shifting data pattern", "pixelated surface sheen" |

## 6. CAMERA & CINEMATOGRAPHY

| Technique | Description | Emotional Effect | Prompt Terms |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|--------------|
| **Anamorphic Lens** | Oval bokeh, horizontal flare, cinematic width | Epic scope, nostalgia, filmic quality | "anamorphic lens", "oval bokeh", "horizontal lens flare" |
| **Neon Bokeh** | Out-of-focus neon becomes abstract color orbs | Dreamlike, abstract, beauty in chaos | "neon bokeh", "abstract color orbs", "dreamlike defocus" |
| **Dutch Angle** | Tilted camera = unease, disorientation, power imbalance | Unease, dystopian tension | "dutch angle", "tilted perspective", "disorienting framing" |
| **Long Lens Isolation** | Telephoto compression isolates subject from environment | Loneliness, surveillance, voyeurism | "telephoto isolation", "compressed urban layers", "voyeuristic framing" |
| **Wide Lens Overwhelm** | Wide angle exaggerates scale, distorts edges | Overwhelming scale, human smallness | "wide angle overwhelm", "distorted perspective", "epic scale" |
| **Practical Light Motivation** | Every light source visible and justified in frame | Realism, grounded dystopia, tactile world | "practical light motivation", "justified light source", "grounded illumination" |
| **Rain as Texture** | Rain catches light, creates streaks, adds motion | Melancholy, cleansing, relentless | "rain as light texture", "water streaks on lens", "relentless precipitation" |

## 9. NEGATIVE PROMPTS

| Avoid | Why | Replace With |
|-------|-----|--------------|
| "natural sunlight" | Cyberpunk has no natural sun | "artificial light only", "neon as sun", "synthetic illumination" |
| "clean and pristine" | Cyberpunk is decayed, worn, lived-in | "technological decay", "lived-in dystopia", "organic wear" |
| "pastel colors" | Cyberpunk is saturated, harsh, neon | "saturated neon", "harsh color contrast", "acid brightness" |
| "rural / natural" | Cyberpunk is urban, artificial, dense | "urban density", "artificial environment", "constructed world" |
| "warm and cozy" | Cyberpunk warmth is synthetic, not genuine | "synthetic warmth", "neon warmth", "artificial comfort" |
| "minimalist" | Cyberpunk is cluttered, information-dense | "visual density", "information overload", "layered clutter" |
