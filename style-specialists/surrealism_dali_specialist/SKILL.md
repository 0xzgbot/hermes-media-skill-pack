## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This skill encodes the complete visual vocabulary of Surrealism — the 20th-century avant-garde movement that sought to channel the unconscious mind. It covers Salvador Dalí's paranoid-critical method, René Magritte's philosophical object displacement, Max Ernst's frottage textures, and the broader Surrealist toolkit: melting clocks, impossible architecture, double images, symbolic objects, and the paradox of hyperrealistic detail applied to irrational subject matter.

When this skill is active, the agent should:
- Use Surrealist-specific terms (paranoid-critical, automatic drawing, exquisite corpse, dream logic)
- Apply the paradox of "photographic precision of impossible scenes"
- Reference Dalí's symbolic vocabulary (soft watches, crutches, ants, drawers)
- Build scenes using illogical juxtaposition and metamorphosis
- Understand the emotional register: uncanny, disorienting, subconscious revelation, dark whimsy

## 2. THE SURREALIST AESTHETIC PHILOSOPHY

Surrealism is not random weirdness — it is **systematic irrationality** with psychological purpose. [^87^] [^90^] [^93^]

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description | Prompt Vocabulary |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Paranoid-Critical Method** | Dalí's technique: deliberately inducing paranoid associations to see multiple images in one form | "paranoid-critical method", "double image", "simultaneous perception", "associative vision" |
| **Illogical Juxtaposition** | Placing ordinary objects in impossible contexts | "illogical juxtaposition", "impossible context", "dislocated object", "dream displacement" |
| **Hyperrealistic Irrationality** | Painting dreams with photographic precision | "hyperrealistic surrealism", "photographic precision of impossible", "meticulous madness" |
| **Metamorphosis** | Objects or bodies transforming into something else | "metamorphosis", "transformation", "morphing form", "becoming other" |
| **Symbolic Language** | Everyday objects imbued with subconscious meaning | "symbolic language", "subconscious symbol", "Freudian object", "psychological emblem" |
| **Dream Logic** | Scenes follow associative, not causal, logic | "dream logic", "associative narrative", "non-causal sequence", "unconscious flow" |
| **The Uncanny** | Familiar made strange; strange made familiar | "uncanny valley", "familiar strangeness", "domestic made alien" |

## 4. COLOR PALETTES

Surrealist color can go in two directions: realistic or symbolic. [^90^]

### Dream Realism (Dalí Style)
- Muted earth tones
- Soft blues and greys
- Natural textures
- Eerily real, like a photograph of a dream

**Prompt:**
> "Dream Realism palette: muted earth tones, soft natural blues, photographic precision, hyperrealistic detail, eerily real dreamscape"

### Subconscious Fire (Emotional Intensity)
- Burning reds
- Deep purples
- Stark gold
- Raw emotion, symbolic intensity

**Prompt:**
> "Subconscious Fire palette: burning red, deep purple, stark gold, raw emotional intensity, symbolic color choice"

### Symbolic Contrast (Logic vs. Chaos)
- Neon brights
- Stark black and white
- Maximum contrast between order and disorder

**Prompt:**
> "Symbolic Contrast palette: neon bright against stark black and white, logic vs. chaos tension, high contrast surrealism"

### Lucid Ocean (Flowing Subconscious)
- Cool blues
- Turquoise
- Soft greys
- Watery, flowing, subconscious movement

**Prompt:**
> "Lucid Ocean palette: cool blue, turquoise, soft grey, watery subconscious flow, liquid dreamscape"

## 5. COMPOSITION TECHNIQUES

| Technique | Description | Prompt Terms |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Paranoid-Critical Double Image** | One form contains two or more images simultaneously | "double image", "simultaneous perception", "figure-ground reversal", "paranoid-critical vision" |
| **Scale Disruption** | Objects at impossible scales — tiny elephants, giant ants | "scale disruption", "impossible proportion", "gigantic miniature", "size paradox" |
| **Gravity Defiance** | Objects floating, water flowing upward, solid objects liquefying | "gravity defiance", "floating object", "upward flow", "solid liquefying" |
| **Architectural Impossibility** | Buildings that could not stand, infinite staircases, Escher-like spaces | "impossible architecture", "infinite staircase", "Escher space", "non-Euclidean building" |
| **Body Dislocation** | Body parts in wrong places, detached limbs, merged bodies | "body dislocation", "detached limb", "merged anatomy", "fragmented figure" |
| **Object Hybridization** | Two unrelated objects fused into one impossible form | "object hybrid", "impossible fusion", "surrealist chimera", "unrelated merge" |
| **Temporal Collapse** | Multiple time periods existing simultaneously | "temporal collapse", "time overlap", "anachronistic coexistence", "past present future" |
| **Atmospheric Distortion** | Sky as liquid, clouds as solid, water as air | "atmospheric distortion", "liquid sky", "solid cloud", "air as water" |
