## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This skill encodes the complete visual vocabulary of Art Nouveau (1890–1910) and Art Deco (1920–1940) — two distinct but related movements that define ornate elegance. Art Nouveau: flowing organic lines, floral motifs, Alphonse Mucha's ethereal women, Gustav Klimt's gold leaf, and nature-as-decoration. Art Deco: geometric precision, sunburst motifs, chrome and steel, ziggurat forms, and the machine-age glamour of Tamara de Lempicka and the Chrysler Building.

When this skill is active, the agent should:
- Use Art Nouveau-specific terms (whiplash curves, arabesque, organic line, stained glass)
- Apply Art Deco-specific terms (chevron, sunburst, stepped forms, chrome, streamline)
- Reference the distinct color palettes (Nouveau: earthy muted pastels; Deco: bold metallic vibrant)
- Build scenes using ornamental architecture and decorative figure composition
- Understand the emotional register: Nouveau = romantic, mystical, natural; Deco = confident, modern, luxurious

## 2. ART NOUVEAU: THE ORGANIC LANGUAGE

Art Nouveau is nature translated into decoration — every line flows, every form grows. [^97^] [^100^]

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description | Prompt Vocabulary |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Organic Line** | Lines curve and flow like vines, hair, water, and flames | "whiplash curve", "organic flowing line", "arabesque", "sinuous form" |
| **Nature as Ornament** | Flowers, insects, birds, and plants become decorative structure | "floral motif", "botanical ornament", "insect detail", "organic decoration" |
| **Asymmetry** | Compositions are deliberately unbalanced, like nature | "asymmetrical composition", "organic imbalance", "natural asymmetry" |
| **Flat Decorative Space** | Shallow depth; figures exist on a decorative plane | "flat decorative space", "shallow depth", "decorative plane" |
| **Ethereal Femininity** | Women are idealized, elongated, surrounded by ornament | "ethereal feminine", "elongated figure", "idealized beauty", "ornate halo" |
| **Hand-Crafted Luxury** | Every detail is artisan-made; no mass production | "hand-crafted detail", "artisan ornament", "bespoke decoration" |

### The Mucha Composition

Alphonse Mucha's poster formula: [^100^]
- **Central female figure:** Ethereal, elongated, idealized, often in profile or three-quarter
- **Ornamental halo/circle:** Behind the figure's head — sun, moon, or floral wreath
- **Vertical decorative borders:** Left and right edges filled with floral or geometric pattern
- **Botanical motifs:** Lilies, roses, ivy, laurel — specific symbolic meaning
- **Title text at bottom:** Integrated into the decorative base
- **Limited palette:** Soft pastels, muted earth tones, gold accents

**Prompt Translation:**
> "Mucha-style composition, ethereal elongated female figure in profile, ornamental circular halo behind head, vertical floral borders left and right, botanical lily and rose motifs, soft pastel palette with gold leaf accents, flat decorative space, hand-crafted artisan detail, asymmetrical organic balance"

## 3. ART DECO: THE GEOMETRIC LANGUAGE

Art Deco is the machine age made beautiful — geometry, speed, luxury, and confidence. [^97^]

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description | Prompt Vocabulary |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Geometric Precision** | Sharp angles, clean lines, perfect symmetry | "geometric precision", "sharp angle", "clean line", "perfect symmetry" |
| **Streamlined Forms** | Aerodynamic curves, speed lines, forward motion | "streamlined form", "aerodynamic curve", "speed line", "forward thrust" |
| **Sunburst & Chevron** | Radiating lines, zigzag patterns, repeating angles | "sunburst motif", "chevron pattern", "radiating line", "zigzag detail" |
| **Luxury Materials** | Chrome, steel, glass, lacquer, exotic woods | "chrome surface", "mirrored glass", "lacquer finish", "exotic wood" |
| **Bold Color & Metallic** | Saturated primaries, gold, silver, black contrast | "bold primary color", "metallic gold", "chrome silver", "high contrast" |
| **Ziggurat & Stepped Forms** | Pyramid-like stacking, tiered architecture | "ziggurat form", "stepped architecture", "tiered structure", "geometric stacking" |
| **Mass Production Elegance** | Beautiful objects made for the modern age | "machine-age elegance", "modern luxury", "industrial beauty" |

### Art Deco Typography
- **Geometric sans-serif:** Futura, Broadway, stencil fonts
- **All caps, wide tracking:** Commands attention
- **Sunburst headlines:** Text radiates from central point
- **Chrome/metallic effect:** Letters look like polished metal
