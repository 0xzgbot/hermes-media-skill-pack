## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This skill encodes the complete visual vocabulary of classical American film noir (1941–1958) — from *The Maltese Falcon* and *Double Indemnity* to *The Third Man* and *Sunset Boulevard*. It covers the specific low-key lighting science, the venetian blind shadow motif, the ethical ambiguity of the noir protagonist, the dangerous allure of the femme fatale, cigarette smoke as atmosphere, rain-slicked urban environments, and the specific high-contrast black-and-white aesthetic that defines the genre.

When this skill is active, the agent should:
- Use noir-specific lighting terms (low-key, hard shadow, venetian blind pattern, key light from below)
- Apply extreme contrast ratios (8:1 to 16:1) with crushed blacks
- Reference specific noir archetypes (detective, femme fatale, corrupt official, fallen hero)
- Build scenes using diagonal compositions and claustrophobic framing
- Understand the emotional register: fatalism, moral ambiguity, desire, dread, cynicism

## 2. THE NOIR AESTHETIC PHILOSOPHY

Film noir is not merely "black and white crime movies" — it is a visual thesis about **moral darkness made visible**.

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description | Prompt Vocabulary |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Low-Key Lighting** | Darkness dominates; light is scarce, harsh, and motivated | "low-key lighting", "hard shadow", "deep black void", "single source" |
| **Venetian Blind Shadows** | Barred light patterns suggesting prison, entrapment, moral cage | "venetian blind shadows", "barred light", "striped shadow pattern" |
| **Moral Ambiguity** | Protagonist is flawed, complicit, or corrupt — not a hero | "flawed protagonist", "moral gray", "cynical expression", "world-weary" |
| **Femme Fatale** | Dangerous beauty who leads men to destruction | "femme fatale", "dangerous beauty", "cigarette holder", "shadowed eyes" |
| **Urban Dread** | City as labyrinth of danger, anonymity, and corruption | "rain-slicked streets", "neon-lit alley", "urban labyrinth" |
| **Smoke Atmosphere** | Cigarette smoke as visible air, texture, and moral haze | "cigarette smoke", "tobacco haze", "smoke catching light" |

## 3. LIGHTING: THE NOIR FORMULA

Noir lighting follows strict rules derived from German Expressionism and Hollywood studio pragmatism.

### The Contrast Ratio Scale

| Ratio | Look | Emotional Effect | Prompt Term |
|-------|------|-----------------|-------------|
| **4:1** | Moderate drama | Standard thriller | "moderate contrast" |
| **8:1** | Classic noir | Moral ambiguity, danger | "high contrast noir" |
| **12:1** | Extreme noir | Desperation, obsession | "extreme low-key" |
| **16:1+** | Expressionist noir | Madness, doom | "crushed blacks, near-total darkness" |

### The Noir Lighting Setup

**Key Light:** Hard, small source — often from below for sinister effect ("monster lighting") or from extreme side
**Fill Light:** Absent or minimal — shadows go to pure black
**Back Light:** Sharp rim separating subject from void
**Background:** Deep black or minimally lit with practical sources
**Shadow Edge:** Hard, sharp, theatrical
**Smoke:** Cigarette smoke or atmospheric haze catching light beams

**Prompt Translation:**
> "Classic film noir low-key lighting, hard single source from extreme side creating sharp shadow edge, deep crushed blacks, 12:1 contrast ratio, cigarette smoke catching light beam, venetian blind shadow bars across face, pure black void background, theatrical hard light, 1940s studio lighting aesthetic"

### The Venetian Blind Motif
- **Source:** Window light passing through horizontal blinds
- **Effect:** Striped shadow bars across face, body, and walls
- **Symbolism:** Prison bars, entrapment, moral cage, fate
- **Variation:** Vertical blinds = bars; diagonal blinds = instability
- **Prompt:** "venetian blind shadows striping across face and wall, barred light pattern, entrapment symbolism"

| Technique | Description | Prompt Terms |

## 5. CHARACTER ARCHETYPES

### The Hard-Boiled Detective
- **Visual:** Fedora pulled low, trench coat collar up, cigarette glowing in darkness
- **Expression:** Cynical, exhausted, knowing too much
- **Lighting:** Half-lit face, one eye in shadow — sees both sides of moral line
- **Pose:** Slouched, leaning against wall, sitting behind desk in pool of lamplight
- **Prompt:** "hard-boiled detective in fedora and trench coat, face half-lit half-shadowed, cigarette glowing orange in darkness, cynical world-weary expression, 1940s urban noir"

### The Femme Fatale
- **Visual:** Sharp cheekbones, red lipstick (even in B&W reads as dark), cigarette holder
- **Expression:** Knowing, dangerous, inviting destruction
- **Lighting:** Key light from below (sinister beauty) or extreme side (half-masked)
- **Pose:** Reclining, legs crossed, smoke rising, looking up through lashes
- **Costume:** Evening gown, gloves, jewelry catching single light source
- **Prompt:** "femme fatale in evening gown, sharp cheekbones and dark lipstick, cigarette holder with smoke rising, dangerous knowing expression, key light from below creating sinister beauty, venetian blind shadows striping across body"

### The Corrupt Official
- **Visual:** Uniform or suit, medals, official regalia
- **Expression:** Smug, predatory, power without morality
- **Lighting:** From below (monster lighting) or harsh overhead (interrogation)
- **Pose:** Behind desk, leaning forward, dominating frame
- **Prompt:** "corrupt official in uniform, face lit from below creating monstrous shadows, smug predatory expression, harsh overhead light, oppressive authority framing"
