## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This skill encodes the complete visual vocabulary of Synthwave (also known as Retrowave, Outrun, or Neon-Noir) — the aesthetic movement that modernizes 1980s and early 1990s visual culture. It covers the iconic neon pink and cyan palette, grid-line landscapes, oversized striped suns, chrome and vector graphics, retro-futuristic vehicles, and the specific nostalgic optimism that distinguishes Synthwave from the dystopian Cyberpunk aesthetic.

When this skill is active, the agent should:
- Use Synthwave-specific terms (grid landscape, vector chrome, sunset gradient, outrun)
- Apply the IBM CGA 4-color palette origin (black, white, magenta, cyan)
- Reference retro computer graphics and early CGI aesthetics
- Build scenes using horizon-line composition and vanishing-point grids
- Understand the emotional register: nostalgic optimism, sunset melancholy, speed, freedom, retro-futuristic wonder

## 2. THE SYNTHWAVE AESTHETIC PHILOSOPHY

Synthwave is not Cyberpunk — it is **nostalgia for a future that never happened**. [^99^]

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description | Prompt Vocabulary |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Nostalgic Optimism** | The 80s imagined a bright technological future; Synthwave mourns that lost optimism | "nostalgic optimism", "lost future", "retro-futuristic wonder", "80s techno-utopia" |
| **Sunset Melancholy** | The sun is always setting — beauty in endings, not dystopia | "sunset melancholy", "dusk beauty", "golden hour nostalgia", "fading light" |
| **Speed and Freedom** | Open roads, fast cars, infinite horizon — escape and possibility | "open road freedom", "speed lines", "infinite horizon", "escape velocity" |
| **Digital Naivety** | Early computer graphics were limited but beautiful — embrace the pixel, the grid, the vector | "digital naivety", "early CGI charm", "vector simplicity", "grid landscape" |
| **Chrome and Glass** | Reflective surfaces, transparency, sleek futurism | "chrome surface", "glass transparency", "reflective futurism", "sleek sheen" |
| **Neon as Nature** | Artificial light replaces the sun; neon is the new daylight | "neon as nature", "artificial daylight", "synthetic sun", "electric warmth" |

## 3. THE SYNTHWAVE COLOR SYSTEM

The iconic Synthwave palette traces back to the **IBM Color Graphics Adapter (1981)** — a 4-color graphics mode in white, black, magenta, and cyan. [^99^]

### The Core Palette

| Color | Hex | Origin | Emotional Use |
|-------|-----|--------|---------------|
| **Neon Magenta** | #FF00FF | IBM CGA, 80s computer graphics | Energy, sunset sky, digital passion |
| **Neon Cyan** | #00FFFF | IBM CGA, early computer screens | Technology, coolness, digital calm |
| **Deep Purple** | #4B0082 | Sunset gradient, twilight transition | Depth, mystery, dusk |
| **Hot Pink** | #FF69B4 | 80s fashion, Miami Vice, synth album art | Glamour, excess, playful energy |
| **Sunset Orange** | #FF4500 | Horizon line, setting sun | Warmth, ending, nostalgic glow |
| **Golden Yellow** | #FFD700 | Sun disc, chrome reflection, optimism | Hope, speed, retro glory |
| **Jet Black** | #0A0A0A | Night sky, grid background, void | Depth, space, digital darkness |
| **Chrome Silver** | #C0C0C0 | Car surfaces, vector lines, futuristic metal | Sleekness, speed, machine beauty |

### The Sunset Gradient
The most iconic Synthwave visual is the **oversized setting sun** with horizontal stripes:
- Top: Deep purple → magenta → hot pink → orange → golden yellow → horizon
- Horizontal banding reminiscent of vintage t-shirt stripes
- Sun occupies 30–50% of the sky
- Always at the horizon, always setting (never rising)

**Prompt Translation:**
> "oversized striped sunset sun at horizon, gradient sky from deep purple through magenta and hot pink to orange and golden yellow, horizontal banding, vintage t-shirt stripe aesthetic, Synthwave sunset"

## 4. THE GRID LANDSCAPE

The grid is Synthwave's defining spatial element — originating from TRON (1982). [^99^]

### Grid Types

| Grid Type | Description | Visual Effect | Prompt Terms |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| **Horizon Grid** | Grid lines converging to vanishing point at horizon | Infinite depth, perspective pull, digital landscape | "horizon grid", "vanishing point grid", "perspective grid lines" |
| **Floor Grid** | Grid on ground plane, often reflecting the sky | Digital ground, virtual space, reflective surface | "floor grid", "ground plane grid", "reflective digital floor" |
| **Mountain Grid** | Grid lines following mountain silhouettes | Nature digitized, landscape as data, geometric terrain | "mountain grid", "geometric terrain", "digitized landscape" |
| **City Grid** | Grid overlaid on urban architecture | City as data, buildings as pixels, urban digitalism | "city grid", "urban digital overlay", "building grid lines" |
| **Particle Grid** | Grid dissolving into particles at edges | Digital dissolution, data fragmentation, ethereal technology | "particle grid", "dissolving grid", "data particle edge" |

### Grid Properties
- **Color:** Usually cyan or magenta, sometimes white
- **Glow:** Soft neon glow on grid lines
- **Perspective:** Strong one-point perspective to horizon
- **Density:** Lines closer together near horizon, wider in foreground
- **Reflection:** Grid reflects on wet or chrome surfaces below

## 7. COMPOSITION & CAMERA

| Technique | Description | Prompt Terms |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Horizon Line Dominance** | Horizon placed low in frame, sky dominates 70% | "low horizon line", "sky dominance", "vast sunset sky" |
| **Vanishing Point Pull** | Strong one-point perspective draws eye to center horizon | "vanishing point pull", "perspective convergence", "depth draw" |
| **Symmetrical Road** | Road or path dead center, leading to horizon | "symmetrical road", "centered path", "leading line to horizon" |
| **Dutch Angle Speed** | Slight tilt suggesting motion, speed, instability | "dutch angle speed", "tilted motion", "dynamic instability" |
| **Low Angle Car** | Camera at bumper level, car dominates frame | "low angle car", "bumper perspective", "car dominance" |
| **Rear View Mirror** | Looking back at sunset through car mirror | "rear view sunset", "mirror reflection", "looking back" |
| **Side Profile Motion** | Car in profile with speed lines, motion blur | "side profile speed", "motion blur lines", "speed streak" |
