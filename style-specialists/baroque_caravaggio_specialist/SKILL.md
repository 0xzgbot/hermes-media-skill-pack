## 3. LIGHTING: THE CARAVAGGIO REVOLUTION

Caravaggio transformed chiaroscuro into **tenebrism** — weaponized light. [^88^] [^91^]

### Chiaroscuro vs. Tenebrism

| Technique | Definition | Visual Characteristic | Emotional Effect |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------|------------------|
| **Chiaroscuro** | Gradual modeling of form through light and shadow | Soft transitions, gentle volume | Naturalism, three-dimensional calm |
| **Tenebrism** | Violent contrast; darkness dominates, light is a harsh spotlight | Sharp edges, deep black voids, isolated illumination | Drama, shock, divine intervention, psychological intensity |

### The Caravaggio Lighting Formula

**Key Light:** Single harsh source from above-left or above-right — often unseen, like a divine spotlight  
**Fill Light:** Absent or minimal — shadows go to pure black  
**Background:** Deep black void — no environment, no context, only the drama  
**Contrast Ratio:** 10:1 to 20:1 — extreme, theatrical  
**Shadow Edge:** Hard, sharp, defined — not soft or gradual  
**Light Color:** Warm, golden, like candlelight or divine radiance  
**Skin Treatment:** Raw, sweaty, dirty, blemished — not idealized

**Prompt Translation:**
> "Caravaggio tenebrism lighting, single harsh spotlight from above-left, deep pure black void background, 10:1 contrast ratio, hard sharp shadow edge, warm golden divine radiance, raw unidealized skin with sweat and blemishes, theatrical stage space, intimate foreground proximity"

### The Calling of Saint Matthew Light
Caravaggio's masterpiece exemplifies the formula: [^88^] [^91^]
- A dim tavern scene suddenly pierced by a beam of divine light
- Light enters from upper right — unseen source, like God reaching down
- Matthew sits in shadow, half-lit, finger pointing to himself in disbelief
- The light creates a diagonal shaft that divides the composition
- Everything outside the light disappears into black void

**Prompt:**
> "Caravaggio Calling of Saint Matthew lighting, divine beam piercing dark tavern from upper right, figures half-lit in shadow, one figure illuminated by sacred light, deep black void outside the beam, diagonal light shaft dividing composition, raw naturalistic figures, intimate stage space"

| Technique | Description | Prompt Terms |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Diagonal Thrust** | Main action line at 45 degrees, creating forward energy | "diagonal thrust", "forward energy", "dynamic diagonal composition" |
| **Intimate Proximity** | Figures close to picture plane, as if viewer is present | "intimate proximity", "foreground emphasis", "viewer presence", "close to picture plane" |

## 5. MATERIALS & TEXTURE

Baroque painters were obsessed with the **tactile reality** of surfaces.

| Material | How It's Painted | Prompt Terms |
|----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Velvet** | Deep folds catch light on edges, pure black in recesses, rich saturated color | "velvet deep fold", "light on fabric edge", "rich saturated textile" |
| **Silk / Satin** | Sharp highlights, smooth gradients, reflective sheen | "silk satin sheen", "sharp fabric highlight", "smooth textile gradient" |
| **Gold Thread / Embroidery** | Tiny bright dots and lines on dark ground, ornate pattern | "gold thread embroidery", "ornate metallic detail", "tiny bright pattern" |
| **Skin** | Raw, blemished, sweaty, translucent in light, pores visible | "raw skin texture", "sweaty translucent flesh", "blemished naturalism", "pore detail" |
| **Metal / Armor** | Sharp reflections, warm highlights, cool shadows, dented and worn | "metal armor reflection", "warm highlight cool shadow", "dented worn surface" |
| **Food / Fruit** | Ripe, decaying, tactile — grapes with bloom, peaches with fuzz | "ripe fruit texture", "decaying organic", "tactile food detail", "grape bloom" |
| **Blood / Wounds** | Wet, glossy, dark red, visceral — not symbolic but real | "wet glossy blood", "visceral wound", "dark red gore", "realistic injury" |

## 6. EMOTIONAL STATES IN BAROQUE ART

| State | Visual Signature | Examples | Prompt Terms |
|-------|-----------------|----------|--------------|
| **Divine Ecstasy** | Head thrown back, eyes rolled upward, mouth open, body limp | Bernini's *Ecstasy of Saint Teresa* | "divine ecstasy", "head thrown back", "eyes rolled up", "body in rapture" |
| **Mortal Terror** | Wide eyes, open mouth, hands raised in defense, body recoiling | Caravaggio's *Judith Beheading Holofernes* | "mortal terror", "wide-eyed fear", "defensive hands", "recoiling body" |
| **Sacred Revelation** | Hand shielding eyes from light, kneeling, awe-struck expression | Caravaggio's *Conversion of Saint Paul* | "sacred revelation", "hand shielding eyes", "kneeling awe", "blinded by light" |
| **Martyrdom** | Serene acceptance, upward gaze, wounds visible but calm | Many saint paintings | "serene martyrdom", "calm acceptance", "wounds with peace" |
| **Worldly Decay** | Skull, rotting fruit, extinguished candle, hourglass | Vanitas paintings | "vanitas symbolism", "skull and decay", "memento mori", "worldly transience" |
