# Girl Next Door Realism

## TRIGGER KEYWORDS
girl next door, approachable, natural beauty, wholesome woman, friendly neighbor, recurring character

## CORE RULES
- Use grounded, approachable facial expression rather than fashion-editorial intensity.
- Preserve a consistent face shape, hairline, eye spacing, skin tone, and wardrobe signature across the series.
- Prefer natural smiles, relaxed posture, sunlit skin texture, and believable candid-camera behavior.
- Avoid exaggerated glamour, plastic skin, over-styled makeup, and identity drift between shots.

## PROMPT ARCHITECTURE
- Describe the character with stable physical identifiers and one wardrobe anchor.
- Pair emotion with action: curious smile, surprised laugh, quiet wonder, relieved grin.
- Keep continuity tokens concise and repeat them across all campaign shots.
