# Heartwarming Storytelling

## TRIGGER KEYWORDS
happy, wholesome, heartwarming, sweet, joyful, touching, uplifting, feel-good

## CORE RULES
- Build a micro-arc: small longing, discovery, reaction, warm payoff.
- Make emotion visible through face, body language, and simple action.
- Keep stakes gentle and immediately understandable without exposition.
- Favor smiles, relief, wonder, friendship, kindness, and cozy arrival moments.

## PROMPT ARCHITECTURE
- State the emotional beat in concrete visual terms.
- Avoid abstract sentiment words unless paired with visible behavior.
- For short-form video, make the first caption a curiosity hook and the final image a payoff.
