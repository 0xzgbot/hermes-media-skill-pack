# SKILL: Product Launch Hero Shot
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Genre & Format Expertise

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of the single-frame or short-sequence product presentation that triggers desire through isolation, material quality revelation, and aspirational positioning. The hero shot is not merely documentation — it is seduction. Every surface, reflection, and shadow must conspire to make the viewer need the object.

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
product shot, hero shot, product launch, product photography, product on white, product floating, product detail, product beauty, packshot, product hero, launch visual, product ad, product commercial, object photography

### CORE RULES
- Product is the protagonist: environment and light serve it completely
- Material specificity matters: render surface texture, finish, and reflectivity precisely
- Isolation creates value: product alone on clean surface or infinite white signals premium
- Shadow placement is intentional: soft drop shadow grounds object; no shadow = floating/premium
- Angles for storytelling: flatlay = context, 3/4 front = form, side = profile, macro = detail obsession
- Reflection is a story: what the product reflects signals the lifestyle it belongs to
- Scale reference when relevant: human hand, coin, or common object establishes product size

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Isolation Strategy:**
- Background: Clean, minimal, or gradient — no competing elements
- Negative Space: Generous space around product = importance, luxury, focus
- Single Subject: Only one product (or one product family in formation)
- Scale: Product fills 30–60% of frame; never tiny, never cramped
- Centering: Centered or golden ratio placement; symmetry for tech, asymmetry for lifestyle
- Floating: Product suspended or on invisible surface = magic, innovation, lightness
- Surface: Reflective surface (glass, water, polished stone) doubles product presence

**Desire Trigger Mechanisms:**
- Tactile Visualization: Lighting and focus make surfaces appear touchable
- Material Honesty: Leather grain, metal machining, glass transparency, fabric weave visible
- Motion Potential: Product appears ready to move, open, activate — kinetic suggestion
- Human Implication: Shadow of hand, partial reflection, or use-context nearby = ownership fantasy
- Scarcity Signal: Single object, pristine condition, perfect lighting = unattainable perfection
- Transformation Promise: Before/after context or upgrade suggestion

**Material Quality Revelation:**
- Lighting Angle: Grazing light (5–15°) reveals surface texture and machining
- Macro Detail: Extreme close-ups of material transitions, seams, buttons, ports
- Reflection Control: Environment reflected in glossy surfaces must be beautiful/abstract
- Color Accuracy: True-to-life color rendering; no color cast distortion
- Shadow Quality: Hard shadows for tech precision; soft shadows for luxury warmth
- Specular Highlights: Controlled bright spots that suggest premium finish

**Cinematographic Approaches:**
- 360° Orbit: Slow rotation around product revealing all angles
- Exploded View: Components separating and reassembling = engineering beauty
- Scale Comparison: Product next to familiar object (hand, coin, phone) for size context
- Environmental Context: Product in aspirational use environment (desk, studio, home)
- Dramatic Entrance: Product emerges from darkness, smoke, or fabric
- Material Transformation: Raw material morphing into finished product

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Floating Monument**
- Setup: Product suspended in dark void with single dramatic light source
- Effect: Object appears weightless, precious, museum-worthy
- Light: Hard rim light + soft fill; or single spotlight from above
- Reflection: Subtle ground reflection suggesting invisible surface
- Emotional: Innovation, future, preciousness, alien technology
- Best For: Tech, watches, jewelry, automotive concept

**2. The Material Origin**
- Setup: Raw material (wood block, metal ingot, leather hide) transitions to finished product
- Effect: Craftsmanship narrative; transformation story
- Light: Warm, revealing texture of both raw and finished states
- Composition: Split frame or morphing sequence
- Emotional: Authenticity, craft, time, value
- Best For: Artisan goods, furniture, fashion, gourmet food

**3. The Tactile Close-Up**
- Setup: Extreme macro of product surface with human finger approaching or touching
- Effect: Triggers tactile mirror neurons; viewer "feels" the surface
- Light: Grazing angle revealing every micro-texture
- Focus: Sharp on contact point; shallow depth beyond
- Emotional: Luxury, sensuality, quality assurance
- Best For: Leather, fabric, metal, wood, premium materials

**4. The Environmental Dream**
- Setup: Product in aspirational but realistic environment
- Effect: Ownership fantasy — "this could be my life"
- Environment: Designer desk, luxury kitchen, creative studio, serene home
- Light: Natural window light or warm practicals; lived-in but perfect
- Placement: Product integrated naturally, not forced
- Emotional: Aspiration, lifestyle upgrade, belonging
- Best For: Home goods, electronics, vehicles, furniture

**5. The Exploded Engineering**
- Setup: Product components separated in space but visually connected
- Effect: Reveals internal complexity and engineering beauty
- Light: Clinical, even, technical; or dramatic with component glow
- Composition: Radial or linear arrangement showing assembly logic
- Motion: Components float into assembly position
- Emotional: Intelligence, precision, innovation, trust
- Best For: Tech, mechanical products, automotive, audio equipment

**6. The Dramatic Reveal**
- Setup: Product hidden by fabric, smoke, shadow, or container
- Sequence: Covering element sweeps away to reveal product in light
- Light: Product illuminated as surroundings remain dark
- Camera: Slow push-in or static with dramatic lighting change
- Emotional: Anticipation, revelation, event, importance
- Best For: Launches, flagship products, luxury, entertainment
