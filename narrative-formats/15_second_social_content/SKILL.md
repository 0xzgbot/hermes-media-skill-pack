# SKILL: 15-Second Social Content
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Genre & Format Expertise

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of ultra-short-form vertical video optimized for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. In 15 seconds, there is no time for setup — the first frame must arrest scrolling, deliver a single crystalline message, and end with loop-friendly energy that makes the viewer watch again. Every frame is a hook; every second is precious [^36^][^46^].

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**The 3-Hook Rule (Visual + Text + Verbal):**
- Visual Hook: First frame must stop the scroll — motion, color, face, or surprise [^46^]
- Text Hook: On-screen text appears in frame 1 (most viewers watch muted) [^46^]
- Verbal Hook: First 3 seconds of audio must deliver value or shock [^36^]
- Combined Power: All three hooks should reinforce same single message

**Hook Types — First 0–3 Seconds:**
- Pattern Interrupt: Unexpected visual (upside-down object, color flash, sudden movement)
- Direct Address: "If you [target audience], watch this" — immediate self-identification [^37^]
- Result Promise: "This fixed my [problem] in 24 hours" — tangible outcome [^36^]
- Visual Shock: Close-up of weird object, bold expression, slow-motion impact [^46^]
- UGC Native: TikTok comment reply format, POV text overlay, "Native" platform style [^37^]
- Question Hook: "Why does no one talk about...?" — curiosity gap [^36^]

**Single Message Discipline:**
- One Idea: 15 seconds can only carry ONE concept, tip, joke, or reveal
- No Subplots: Delete any secondary information
- Visual-First: Message should be understandable without audio
- Text Overlay: Maximum 5–8 words on screen at once; 2-second minimum per text card
- End Frame: Final frame restates hook or delivers punchline for loop continuity

**Loop-Friendly Structure:**
- Seamless End: Final frame visually or audibly connects to opening frame
- Repetitive Beat: Music or motion that loops without jarring cut
- Open Loop: End with question or unresolved moment that demands rewatch
- Punchline Return: Final image echoes opening image with transformation
- Energy Match: End energy = start energy (no fade to black)

**Platform Technical Specs:**
- Aspect Ratio: 9:16 vertical (1080×1920)
- Safe Zone: Keep text and key visuals in center 60% (edges may be cropped by UI)
- Text Placement: Avoid bottom 15% (caption overlay area) [^37^]
- Pace: 1 cut per 1–2 seconds minimum; never let frame stagnate [^37^]
- Sound: Trending audio or original sound with clear first 3 seconds

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Pattern Interrupt Opener**
- Frame 1: Upside-down product, person entering from unusual angle, color inversion
- Seconds 1–3: Visual confusion resolved into message
- Seconds 4–12: Single concept delivery with text reinforcement
- Seconds 13–15: Punchline or transformation
- Loop: End image mirrors start image but corrected
- Best For: Product demos, magic tricks, transformations

**2. The UGC Comment Reply**
- Frame 1: Screenshot of TikTok comment: "Does this actually work? 🤨"
- Text Overlay: "Let's test it right now"
- Seconds 3–12: Demonstration of product/tip/technique
- Seconds 13–15: Result reveal with reaction
- Loop: Result image invites rewatch to see process again [^37^]
- Best For: Product reviews, life hacks, tutorials

**3. The POV Immersion**
- Frame 1: Text "POV: You're [relatable scenario]"
- Visual: First-person perspective of situation
- Seconds 3–12: Scenario plays out with escalating detail
- Seconds 13–15: Punchline or relatable resolution
- Loop: Scenario resets to opening moment [^37^]
- Best For: Comedy, relatable content, skits, workplace humor

**4. The Result Promise**
- Frame 1: Text "I [achieved result] in [timeframe]"
- Seconds 1–3: Before state or problem identification
- Seconds 4–12: Process montage (fast cuts)
- Seconds 13–15: After result with proof
- Loop: Before/after comparison invites rewatch [^36^]
- Best For: Fitness, finance, productivity, transformations

**5. The Visual Metaphor**
- Frame 1: Abstract or symbolic image (not literal)
- Seconds 3–8: Metaphor develops with motion
- Seconds 9–12: Connection to real message made clear
- Seconds 13–15: Brand or call-to-action
- Loop: Symbolic image returns, now understood
- Best For: Brand messaging, emotional content, awareness campaigns

### TECHNICAL NOTES FOR AI GENERATION
- Always specify "vertical 9:16" or "TikTok/Reels format" for aspect ratio
- Include "first frame" or "frame 1" instructions explicitly
- Use "bold text overlay" and specify word count (5–8 words)
- Mention "fast cuts" or "jump cuts" for pacing
- Include "loop-friendly" for seamless end-to-beginning connection
- Specify "center safe zone" to prevent UI cropping
- Use "high contrast" or "saturated" for thumb-stopping color
- Mention "native aesthetic" or "UGC style" for platform-appropriate look
