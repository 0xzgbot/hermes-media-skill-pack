# Sunlit Travel Cinematography

## TRIGGER KEYWORDS
travel, destination, wanderlust, sunlit, vacation, scenic, road trip, arrival, viewpoint

## CORE RULES
- Use warm directional sunlight, clean sky bounce, and readable depth layers.
- Start social shorts with a human-scale travel moment before the wide postcard reveal.
- Keep landmarks and horizon lines stable in vertical crops.
- Use motion cues such as walking into frame, hair moving in breeze, handheld push-in, or reveal pan.

## PROMPT ARCHITECTURE
- Combine person, destination clue, and emotional reason for being there.
- Use vertical depth: foreground detail, character midground, destination background.
- Avoid empty postcard shots unless they pay off a character moment.
