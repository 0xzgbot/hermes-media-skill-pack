### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Time-Lapse Fundamentals:**
- **Interval:** Time between frames determines speed — 1s interval = 24h in 1 minute
- **Exposure:** Each frame must be properly exposed; changing light requires auto or ramping
- **Motion Blur:** Slow shutter (1/2 to 2 seconds) per frame creates fluid motion streaks
- **Stability:** Locked tripod essential; any movement magnifies dramatically
- **Duration:** Hours or days of real time compressed to seconds
- **Emotional Register: Awe, scale, patience, inevitability, life continuing**

**Hyper-Lapse Fundamentals:**
- **Movement:** Camera travels significant distance between each frame
- **Registration:** Same focal point locked frame-to-frame for smooth motion
- **Interval:** 1–3 seconds of movement between shots
- **Path:** Straight lines, arcs, or complex trajectories through space
- **Stitching:** Hundreds of still frames become fluid camera movement
- **Emotional Register: Impossible perspective, god-like movement, architectural intimacy**

**Time-Lapse Subject Categories:**

| Subject | Interval | Best Time | Visual Effect | Emotional |
|---------|---------|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Clouds** | 2–5 seconds | Any | Sky rivers, weather fronts | Nature's power, scale |
| **Stars** | 15–30 seconds | Clear night | Earth rotation visible | Cosmic perspective, insignificance |
| **Traffic** | 1–2 seconds | Rush hour | Light rivers, urban pulse | City as organism, energy |
| **Construction** | 5–30 minutes | Daylight | Building growing from ground | Creation, progress, patience |
| **Plant Growth** | 5–30 minutes | Daylight | Life unfurling | Nature's persistence, time |
| **Crowds** | 1–2 seconds | Events | Human tide, patterns | Society, collective behavior |
| **Day-to-Night** | 10–30 seconds | Sunset | Sky color gradient | Transition, cycle, eternity |
| **Tides** | 1–2 seconds | Coastal | Water advancing/retreating | Rhythm, inevitability, change |

**Hyper-Lapse Path Types:**
- **Straight Push:** Camera moves directly toward/away from subject
- **Orbit:** Circular path around building, statue, or landmark
- **Through Space:** Camera weaves through corridors, streets, or structures
- **Vertical Rise:** Crane-up effect from ground to aerial perspective
- **Follow Surface:** Camera skims along ground, water, or wall

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Holy Grail Day-to-Night**
- Technique: Exposure ramping from day through sunset to night
- Sky: Blue → orange → magenta → deep blue → black with stars
- City: Invisible → golden hour lit → twilight silhouette → electric night
- Motion Blur: Increasing as light fades, creating light rivers at night
- Emotional: The eternal cycle, patience rewarded, city awakening
- Best For: City films, brand heritage, travel, epic establishing shots

**2. The Star Trails Rotation**
- Technique: 15–30 second intervals over 4+ hours
- Effect: Stars become circular trails around celestial pole
- Foreground: Static landscape or silhouette anchor
- Motion: Earth's rotation made visible
- Emotional: Cosmic scale, human insignificance, ancient wonder
- Best For: Nature brands, contemplative content, science, awe

**3. The Urban Hyper-Lapse Orbit**
- Path: Perfect circle around landmark or building
- Distance: 50–200 meters circumference
- Registration: Building center locked in same frame position
- Result: Building appears static while world spins around it
- Emotional: Architectural power, human achievement, dizzying scale
- Best For: Architecture, real estate, city branding, monuments

**4. The Construction Genesis**
- Interval: 10–30 minutes over weeks or months
- Subject: Building rising from foundation to completion
- Technique: Same locked framing throughout
- Result: Structure grows like living organism
- Emotional: Creation, progress, investment, future
- Best For: Corporate heritage, infrastructure, development, industry

**5. The Nature Bloom**
- Interval: 5–15 minutes over days
- Subject: Flower opening, plant growing, ice melting, season changing
- Technique: Controlled environment, consistent lighting
- Result: Invisible life processes made visible
- Emotional: Nature's persistence, fragility, beauty, time
- Best For: Environmental brands, wellness, agriculture, science

### TECHNICAL NOTES FOR AI GENERATION
- Use "time-lapse" for static camera temporal compression
- Use "hyper-lapse" for moving camera through compressed space
- Specify motion blur: "streaking clouds" or "light trails" for fluid effect
- For FLUX: describe single representative frame with temporal context — "time-lapse frame showing cloud streaks"
- For LTX: describe motion explicitly — "time-lapse acceleration of clouds racing across sky"
- Day-to-night transitions require describing full light range
- Hyper-lapses need registration point: "building remains centered as camera orbits"
- Combine with wide lens (16–24mm) for maximum scale and coverage
