# SKILL: Macro Intimacy
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Camera & Lens Language

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of extreme close-up photography using dedicated macro optics or close-focusing systems. Macro reveals microscopic worlds at 1:1 magnification or greater, rendering tiny subjects life-size or larger on sensor. Creates profound intimacy through texture revelation, scale manipulation, and the abstraction of familiar objects into alien landscapes. Essential for nature, product, scientific, and surrealist work.

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
macro, extreme close-up, close up, 1:1 magnification, micro, detail shot, texture reveal, insect close-up, petal close-up, eye macro, macro photography, intimate detail, magnified, surface texture, product detail

### CORE RULES
- 1:1 magnification is the macro threshold: subject life-size on sensor
- Depth of field at macro distances is measured in millimeters — precise focus is everything
- Texture becomes the subject: familiar surfaces become alien landscapes at macro scale
- Diffused ring light or side-lighting at very low angle reveals maximum surface detail
- Any vibration is fatal: specify tripod or stabilization for macro work
- Scale reference helps viewer: include a familiar element to show true size of subject
- Bokeh at macro distances creates abstract color fields behind the sharp subject

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Magnification Ratios:**
- Close-Up: 1:4 to 1:2 — Small subjects rendered smaller than life-size
- Macro: 1:1 — Subject rendered life-size on sensor (e.g., 24mm insect = 24mm on sensor)
- Extreme Macro: 2:1 to 5:1+ — Subject rendered larger than life-size
- Anamorphic Macro: 1:4 magnification with anamorphic character and oval bokeh [^24^]

**Working Distance:**
- Definition: Distance from lens front element to subject
- Trade-off: Higher magnification = shorter working distance
- 1:1 at 100mm: ~12 inches working distance
- 1:1 at 50mm: ~6 inches working distance
- Lighting Challenge: Short distance blocks ambient light; requires specialized illumination

**Depth of Field Crisis:**
- Problem: At 1:1 magnification, depth of field shrinks to fractions of a millimeter
- f/2.8 at 1:1: ~0.1mm depth of field
- f/16 at 1:1: ~1mm depth of field (still extremely thin)
- Solution: Focus stacking (multiple images merged), tilt-shift techniques, or acceptance of shallow DOF as aesthetic

**Texture Revelation:**
- Surface Detail: Skin pores, insect eyes, fabric weave, metal grain, plant cells
- Light Interaction: Grazing light essential for texture (raking angle 5–15°)
- Specular Response: Microscopic highlights reveal surface topology
- Abstraction: Familiar objects become unrecognizable alien terrain at high magnification

**Scale Manipulation:**
- Small as Large: Ant rendered as grizzly bear scale
- Large as Abstract: Eye becomes landscape of iris canyons
- Context Removal: No reference objects = scale ambiguity
- Surreal Effect: Viewer cannot determine actual size of subject

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. Eye Macro (Windows to Soul)**
- Magnification: 1:1 to 2:1
- Focus Plane: Iris texture (crypts, furrows, pigment variations)
- Catchlight: Small, precise reflection of light source
- Surrounding: Skin pores, eyelash detail, tear film surface
- Emotional: Intimacy, vulnerability, biological truth
- Technical: Ring light or twin flash for even illumination

**2. Insect Alien Landscape**
- Magnification: 1:1 to 3:1
- Subject: Compound eyes, wing veins, antennae segments
- Lighting: Diffused flash to freeze motion; dark field for transparency
- Background: Pure black or environmental blur
- Emotional: Alien beauty, biological engineering, otherworldly
- Technical: Focus stacking essential for compound eye depth

**3. Botanical Abstraction**
- Magnification: 1:2 to 2:1
- Subject: Pollen, stamens, leaf veins, dew drops
- Lighting: Backlight for translucency; side-light for texture
- Water: Dew drops act as lenses, refracting background
- Emotional: Fragile beauty, hidden worlds, nature's complexity
- Technical: Natural light or diffused LED panel

**4. Product Texture Hero**
- Magnification: 1:4 to 1:1
- Subject: Watch gears, fabric weave, leather grain, metal machining
- Lighting: Controlled grazing light to reveal craftsmanship
- Depth: Accept shallow DOF as luxury aesthetic
- Emotional: Craft, quality, attention to detail, luxury
- Technical: Copy stand, tethered shooting, focus stacking

**5. Water Drop Universe**
- Magnification: 1:1 to 3:1
- Subject: Water droplets on surface, containing refracted world
- Effect: Each drop becomes wide-angle lens showing inverted environment
- Background: Colorful or textured surface visible through drops
- Emotional: Microcosm, fragility, physics as poetry
- Technical: High speed flash to freeze drop motion

**6. Skin Truth Portrait**
- Magnification: 1:2 to 1:1
- Subject: Skin texture, pores, fine hairs, wrinkles
- Lighting: Grazing side-light revealing topography
- Emotional: Raw humanity, mortality, authenticity, time
- Technical: Requires model comfort and trust; no retouching
- Context: Challenge beauty standards through biological truth
