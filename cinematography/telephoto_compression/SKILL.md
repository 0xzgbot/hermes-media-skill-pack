# SKILL: Telephoto Compression
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Camera & Lens Language

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of long focal lengths (85mm–600mm+) that compress spatial relationships, flatten perspective, and isolate subjects against backgrounds. Telephoto lenses make distant objects appear closer to foreground subjects than reality, creating layered, dense compositions. Essential for portraits, sports, wildlife, crowd scenes, and abstracting environments into graphic patterns [^26^].

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
telephoto, long lens, compression, 85mm, 135mm, 200mm, 300mm, background compression, flattened perspective, bokeh background, portrait lens, sports photography, wildlife photography, compressed layers, shallow depth, distant subject

### CORE RULES
- Telephoto compression flattens spatial distance: background appears closer than reality
- Distance from camera creates compression, not focal length alone — back up and zoom in
- Background at telephoto distances becomes painterly abstract field of color/light (bokeh)
- Portrait sweet spot: 85mm–135mm for flattering facial proportions without distortion
- Crowds become graphic patterns: individual faces compress into repeated textures
- Sun/moon appear larger relative to foreground subjects the longer the focal length
- Surveillance/voyeuristic framing: telephoto creates distance between viewer and subject

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Focal Length Categories:**
- Short Telephoto: 85–135mm — Portrait standard, moderate compression
- Medium Telephoto: 135–300mm — Significant compression, sports/wildlife
- Super Telephoto: 300–600mm+ — Extreme compression, distant subjects loom large
- FOV Narrowing: 85mm = 28° diagonal; 600mm = 4° diagonal

**Compression Effect Physics:**
- Mechanism: Narrow FOV crops wide scene; distant objects fill more frame than wide lens
- Perception: Background appears closer to subject than actual spatial relationship
- Distance Factor: Greater subject-to-background distance = more dramatic compression [^26^]
- Overlap Effect: Multiple subjects at different distances appear stacked/layered [^26^]
- Scale Contrast: Small foreground + large distant object creates surreal scale (moon/buildings)

**Background Separation Strategies:**
- Aperture: Wide open (f/2.8–f/4) for shallow depth of field + compression
- Distance: Maximize subject-to-background separation
- Bokeh: Telephoto lenses produce smooth, creamy bokeh due to narrow FOV
- Focal Length Rule: Longer lens = more background blur at same aperture and distance

**Crowd & Environment Compression:**
- Effect: Dense crowds appear even denser; individuals stack into pattern
- Urban: Buildings line up into flat graphic layers
- Nature: Mountain ridges compress into stacked silhouettes
- Sports: Athletes appear closer to each other than reality

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. Portrait Compression (85–135mm)**
- Focal Length: 85mm (natural perspective); 135mm (flattering flattening)
- Effect: Facial features compress pleasingly; nose doesn't project excessively
- Background: Cityscape, nature, or architecture compressed behind subject
- Bokeh: Smooth, circular, creamy at f/1.8–f/2.8
- Emotional: Intimate but not invasive; subject isolated from world

**2. Extreme Background Looming (300mm+)**
- Setup: Subject in foreground with distant landmark/mountain/moon
- Effect: Background object appears gigantic relative to subject
- Distance: Subject-to-camera moderate; subject-to-background very large
- Emotional: Surreal, overwhelming, nature's dominance, human insignificance
- Example: Person in street with mountain appearing to tower immediately behind [^26^]

**3. Crowd Density Abstraction**
- Focal Length: 200–400mm
- Setup: Shooting across crowd at slight angle
- Effect: Individuals compress into overlapping pattern; density amplified
- Layering: Foreground heads + midground shoulders + background faces merge
- Emotional: Urban overwhelm, anonymity, social density, isolation in crowds
- Best For: City streets, concerts, protests, markets

**4. Stacked Landscape Layers**
- Focal Length: 100–300mm
- Setup: Distant landscape with multiple depth planes
- Effect: Hills, ridges, or buildings compress into flat graphic strips
- Atmosphere: Haze between layers enhances separation
- Emotional: Mystical, contemplative, Japanese painting aesthetic
- Best For: Mountain ridges, city skylines, forest layers

**5. Sports Action Isolation**
- Focal Length: 200–600mm
- Setup: Distant athlete isolated from chaotic background
- Effect: Subject fills frame while crowd compresses into colored backdrop
- Shutter: Fast (1/1000+) to freeze motion
- Emotional: Heroic isolation, focus under pressure, peak moment

**6. Urban Graphic Compression**
- Focal Length: 100–200mm
- Setup: Street scene with buildings lining up
- Effect: Signs, streetlights, people compress into dense flat pattern
- Angle: Slightly elevated for maximum overlap
- Emotional: Urban jungle, sensory overload, city as organism
