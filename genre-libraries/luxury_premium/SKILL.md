# SKILL: Luxury & Premium
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Brand Voice & Style

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of the restrained visual language that signals extreme value through absence rather than abundance. Luxury cinematography whispers where mass-market shouts. It relies on negative space, material obsession, slow time, and the deliberate withholding of information. Every frame must feel expensive — not because of what is shown, but because of what is left out.

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
luxury, premium, high-end, expensive, exclusive, elite, opulent, refined, prestige, luxury brand, high fashion, bespoke, artisan quality, couture, fine art, expensive aesthetic, understated luxury

### CORE RULES
- Negative space is value: empty space around subject signals that space costs something
- Restraint over abundance: fewer elements = higher perceived value
- Material obsession: name every surface and its specific quality — "matte brushed titanium", not "metal"
- Color palette is desaturated or muted: vivid saturation signals mass-market; luxury is tonal
- Silence in composition: no competing elements, no visual noise, nothing unnecessary
- Time is slow: luxury content implies deliberate pace — never rushed composition
- Detail at extreme close-up: stitching, grain, facet — the obsessive detail justifies cost

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Restraint Architecture:**
- Cut Rate: 3–6 cuts per 60 seconds; time moves slowly
- Shot Duration: 5–10 second minimum per shot; let the image breathe
- Camera Movement: Slow, deliberate, often static; gimbal drift rather than active motion
- Color Palette: Muted, desaturated, or monochromatic; no primary colors unless signature brand red
- Sound Design: Sparse; silence as luxury; ambient room tone or single instrument
- Text Density: Minimal or absent; logo appears once, quietly
- End Frame: Hold on product or scene for 3–5 seconds of silence

**Negative Space as Value:**
- Frame Composition: Subject occupies 20–40% of frame; environment is void
- Background: Solid dark, gradient, or out-of-focus minimalism
- Breathing Room: Generous headroom and side space = room for thought, room for money
- Isolation: Single object or single figure; never cluttered
- Scale: Small subject in vast space = preciousness, rarity, museum quality

**Material Obsession:**
- Macro Priority: Surface texture is the story — leather grain, metal machining, stone veining, silk weave
- Grazing Light: 5–15° side light reveals micro-texture; every scratch and pore is intentional
- Specular Control: Tight, precise highlights suggesting hand-polished surfaces
- Color Temperature: Warm for heritage (2800K–3500K); cool for modern luxury (4500K–5500K)
- Reflection Quality: Environment reflected in glossy surfaces must be beautiful/abstract
- Tactile Sound: Implied audio of material — leather creak, metal click, fabric rustle

**Time & Gravity:**
- Slow Motion: Water droplets, fabric movement, hand gestures at 120fps+
- Stillness: Subject motionless while environment subtly moves (curtain, smoke, dust)
- Weight: Objects placed with deliberate gravity; nothing casual or accidental
- Heritage Time: Visual suggestion of decades or centuries (patina, wear, tradition)

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Void Reveal**
- Setup: Pure black or white void; product emerges slowly into light
- Movement: Product rotates or light sweeps across surface
- Duration: 10–15 seconds of near-nothingness before full reveal
- Emotional: Anticipation, preciousness, event, scarcity
- Best For: Watch launches, jewelry, fragrance, automotive concept

**2. Material Autopsy**
- Setup: Extreme macro traveling across product surface
- Light: Grazing, raking, revealing every micro-detail
- Movement: Slow lateral drift; no cuts for 10+ seconds
- Sound: Material-specific audio (leather creak, metal resonance)
- Emotional: Craft, time, obsession, worthiness
- Best For: Leather goods, watches, furniture, bespoke tailoring

**3. The Heirloom Frame**
- Setup: Object in context of generations — old hands, antique environment
- Light: Warm, golden, window light suggesting afternoon and memory
- Color: Sepia-tinged or warm desaturation
- Texture: Patina, wear, use-history as beauty
- Emotional: Legacy, inheritance, timelessness, family
- Best For: Heritage brands, whiskey, estate jewelry, luxury real estate

**4. Architectural Containment**
- Setup: Subject in vast architectural space (museum, gallery, palace, desert)
- Scale: Human or product small against monumental geometry
- Light: Natural through massive windows; or dramatic single source
- Movement: Slow tracking shot revealing scale relationship
- Emotional: Awe, permanence, cultural weight, achievement
- Best For: Real estate, automotive, fashion couture, art

**5. The Single Gesture**
- Setup: One hand performing one action with infinite deliberation
- Action: Fastening clasp, pouring liquid, adjusting cuff, opening box
- Framing: Tight on hand and object; face not needed
- Speed: Slow motion or real-time with gravitas
- Emotional: Ritual, care, intention, privilege
- Best For: Accessories, fragrance, spirits, beauty, watches

**6. Modern Minimal**
- Setup: Clean geometric space, concrete/glass/white
- Light: Cool, even, precise; clinical but beautiful
- Product: Singular, centered, perfect
- Color: Black, white, silver, with single brand accent
- Emotional: Future, precision, innovation, quiet confidence
- Best For: Tech luxury, skincare, modern furniture, concept stores
