# SKILL: Health & Wellness
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Brand Voice & Style

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of the airy, luminous visual language that signals restoration, balance, and gentle transformation. Wellness cinematography breathes — it moves slowly, glows softly, and integrates the human subject with natural elements. The frame must lower the viewer's heart rate while suggesting the possibility of becoming one's best self. Nature is not background; it is co-therapist [^55^].

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
health, wellness, yoga, mindfulness, meditation, calm, nature, healing, balance, mental health, spa, clean living, self-care, holistic, fitness wellness, breathe, restoration

### CORE RULES
- Luminosity is emotional: soft backlight + diffused fill creates the signature glow
- Color palette is cool-to-neutral: sage green, warm white, clay, sky blue — never harsh saturated
- Slow pacing implied in static frames: subject at rest, mid-breath, contemplative
- Nature integration is essential: skin touching earth, sunlight through leaves, water nearby
- Negative space signals peace: frames are never cluttered; breathing room is structural
- Subject's expression: not performative happiness — quiet contentment, inward peace
- Texture of natural materials (linen, wood, stone, water) grounds the aspirational in the real

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Light Airiness:**
- Exposure: Slightly overexposed by 0.5–1 stop; highlights bloom gently
- Key Light: Soft, diffused, wrapping — large sources or natural cloud cover
- Fill: Generous; shadows are open and luminous, not dark
- Backlight: Strong rim light separating subject from environment = ethereal, angelic
- Color Temperature: Warm-to-neutral (4000K–5500K); avoid cool clinical unless medical context
- Atmosphere: Haze, mist, steam, dust particles visible in light beams
- Lens: Soft filtration or vintage lens for gentle halation around highlights

**Nature Integration:**
- Environment: Forest, ocean, mountain, garden, desert — natural space as active participant
- Subject Scale: Human figure integrated into landscape, not dominating it
- Elements: Water, stone, wood, plants, sand, snow — tactile natural materials
- Season: Spring and autumn preferred (renewal and harvest metaphors)
- Weather: Soft rain, morning mist, dappled sunlight, gentle wind
- Architecture: Natural materials — wood, stone, clay, bamboo; biophilic design
- Animals: Birds, deer, butterflies, horses — wild but gentle presence

**Calm Palette:**
- Primary: Sage green, soft blue, warm sand, cream, white
- Secondary: Terracotta, muted lavender, pale gold, stone gray
- Avoid: Saturated primaries, neon, harsh red, aggressive orange
- Earth Tones: Grounded, organic, human — browns and greens from soil and leaf [^55^]
- Water Tones: Aquatic blues and greens suggesting flow and purity
- Skin Rendering: Warm, healthy, luminous; no harsh contrast on faces

**Movement & Pacing:**
- Speed: Slow motion for water, breath, hair, fabric; real-time for walking, yoga, meditation
- Camera: Floating, drifting, gentle push-in; never jarring or whip-pan
- Duration: Longer takes; 5–10 seconds minimum to allow breath
- Subject Motion: Fluid, continuous, circular — no sharp angles or sudden stops
- Transitions: Dissolves, match cuts on motion, gentle fades

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Morning Ritual**
- Setup: Subject in natural morning light performing wellness routine
- Light: Sunrise through window or outdoor dawn; golden-pink soft
- Action: Yoga, stretching, tea preparation, journaling, meditation
- Environment: Minimal natural space — wood floor, linen, plants, stone
- Pace: Slow, deliberate, unhurried; time is abundant
- Emotional: Renewal, discipline, self-care, beginning
- Best For: Apps, supplements, skincare, fitness, mindfulness

**2. Forest Bathing (Shinrin-yoku)**
- Setup: Subject walking slowly through old-growth forest
- Light: Dappled sunlight through canopy; green-gold filtering
- Camera: Slow tracking or static; subject moves through frame
- Scale: Human small among massive trees; humility and connection
- Sound: Implied — rustling leaves, bird song, footfall on moss
- Emotional: Grounding, ancestral memory, ecological belonging
- Best For: Retreats, mental health, eco-products, travel, insurance

**3. The Water Cure**
- Setup: Subject in or near water — ocean, lake, hot spring, rain
- Light: Reflected, shimmering, backlit through water
- Action: Floating, swimming, bathing, walking in surf
- Texture: Water droplets on skin, steam rising, ripples, waves
- Color: Aquatic blues, greens, whites; skin glowing
- Emotional: Purity, release, flow, emotional cleansing
- Best For: Spas, hydration, skincare, travel, mental health

**4. The Healing Touch**
- Setup: Hands performing care — massage, applying oil, holding herbs, planting
- Camera: Close, intimate, respectful distance
- Light: Warm, soft, golden; window or candle-quality
- Texture: Skin, oil, plant material, soil, fabric
- Pace: Slow motion; every gesture meaningful
- Emotional: Care, expertise, tradition, tenderness
- Best For: Skincare, spa, therapy, organic products, medicine

**5. The Breath Space**
- Setup: Subject in meditation or breathwork
- Environment: Minimal natural space or pure nature
- Camera: Static for 10+ seconds; only breath moves
- Light: Soft, even, luminous; subject gently illuminated
- Atmosphere: Incense smoke, steam, or dust catching light
- Emotional: Peace, presence, inward journey, stillness
- Best For: Meditation apps, therapy, retreats, supplements, mindfulness

**6. The Transformation Garden**
- Setup: Before/after or growth metaphor in natural setting
- Visual: Seed to plant, bud to bloom, cocoon to butterfly, dawn to day
- Light: Shifting from dim to bright, cool to warm
- Pace: Time-lapse or slow reveal
- Subject: Observing or participating in transformation
- Emotional: Growth, hope, patience, natural cycles
- Best For: Recovery, personal development, beauty, nutrition, education
