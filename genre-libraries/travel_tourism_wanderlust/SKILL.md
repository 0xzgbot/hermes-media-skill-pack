# SKILL: Travel & Tourism Wanderlust
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Destination Cinematography

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of destination cinematography, cultural visual storytelling, and aspirational travel imagery for AI-generated campaigns. This skill encodes the grammar of wanderlust: the golden hour landmark shot, the respectful cultural gaze, the drone establishing reveal, the food-and-tradition B-roll logic, and the specific emotional architecture that makes viewers feel they MUST visit this place. Essential for tourism boards, travel brands, hospitality, and any campaign selling the transformative promise of place.

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**The 5-Shot Destination Sequence:**
1. **The Aerial Establishing:** Drone or high vantage — context, scale, geography
2. **The Landmark Icon:** Hero shot of signature monument/landscape at magic hour
3. **The Human Moment:** Local person in authentic environment — culture, not costume
4. **The Sensory Detail:** Food, texture, craft, nature macro — tactile desire
5. **The Aspirational Figure:** Traveler experiencing the moment — viewer surrogate

**Golden Hour Landmark Rules:**
- **Timing:** 20 minutes before sunset or after sunrise for raking light on facades
- **Position:** Light source behind or to side of camera for facade illumination
- **Sky:** Include colorful sky as compositional element — 30–50% of frame
- **Foreground:** Use local elements (flowers, street, water) for depth and place specificity
- **Reflection:** Water doubles the shot's impact when available
- **Crowd Management:** Early morning guarantees emptiness; sunset requires patience

**Cultural Respect Framework:**
- **People:** Photographed as individuals with dignity, not exotic props
- **Sacred Spaces:** Modest framing, no posing on altars, appropriate dress visible
- **Ceremony:** Observer position, not participant; never interrupt
- **Children:** Only with context of family/community; never isolated
- **Poverty:** Never romanticized; dignity maintained in all economic conditions
- **Prompt Signal:** "respectful documentary gaze", "dignified portrayal", "authentic context"

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Landmark Awakening**
- Setup: Famous monument at first light, empty, dew on ground
- Light: Warm horizontal rays hitting facade, long shadows
- Sky: Soft pink to gold gradient
- Foreground: Local flowers or cobblestones with dew drops
- Emotional: Possibility, discovery, the place before the crowds
- Best For: Luxury travel, heritage tourism, iconic destinations

**2. The Human Scale**
- Setup: Local person in their authentic environment
- Framing: Environmental portrait — person small in place context
- Light: Natural, available, respectful
- Expression: Dignified, working, present — not performing for camera
- Context: Their craft, home, shop, or landscape visible
- Emotional: Connection, shared humanity, respect, curiosity
- Best For: Cultural tourism, documentary travel, sustainable tourism

**3. The Food Pilgrimage**
- Setup: Local dish in authentic preparation or presentation environment
- Framing: Overhead or 45° — shows ingredients and context
- Light: Warm, appetizing, often practical (market, kitchen, fire)
- Detail: Steam rising, sauce glistening, hands preparing
- Context: Market, kitchen, street cart, family table
- Emotional: Hunger, curiosity, cultural immersion, ritual
- Best For: Food tourism, culinary brands, cooking shows, hospitality

**4. The Impossible View**
- Setup: Vantage point most travelers never reach
- Technique: Drone, mountain peak, helicopter, rooftop access
- Perspective: God's-eye view or extreme elevation
- Scale: Human figure tiny against vast landscape
- Emotional: Awe, insignificance, achievement, privilege
- Best For: Adventure travel, luxury expedition, National Geographic style

**5. The Seasonal Transformation**
- Setup: Same location across seasons (sakura, autumn, snow, summer)
- Technique: Matching framing for seasonal comparison
- Color: Cherry pink, autumn amber, winter white, summer green
- Emotional: Time passing, return visits, endless discovery
- Best For: Repeat visitation campaigns, annual events, year-round destinations

### TECHNICAL NOTES FOR AI GENERATION
- Use specific place names for stronger visual priors: "Kyoto", "Santorini", "Patagonia"
- Specify time of day precisely — travel photography is all about light timing
- Include local elements in foreground for place specificity
- For FLUX: describe in positive aspirational terms; no negative prompts
- For LTX: specify "stable landmark composition across frames" as guardrail
- Respectful portrayal is essential — mention "dignified", "authentic context", "respectful gaze"
- Scale contrast (tiny human / vast landscape) creates wanderlust most effectively
- Seasonal specificity (sakura, autumn leaves, snow) anchors the image in time
