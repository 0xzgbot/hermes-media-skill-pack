# SKILL: Automotive
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Brand Voice & Style

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of the high-velocity, high-drama visual language that transforms vehicles from transportation into desire machines. Automotive cinematography balances mechanical precision with environmental spectacle — the car is both sculpture and protagonist, capable of dominating any landscape it enters. Power must be visible in sheet metal, sound must be implied in motion blur, and aspiration must radiate from every reflection.

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
automotive, car, vehicle, automobile, sports car, luxury car, car commercial, driving, race car, SUV, electric vehicle, car photography, car cinematography, car ad, speed, horsepower, chrome, sheet metal

### CORE RULES
- Vehicle is the protagonist: it has presence, energy, and narrative weight like a lead character
- Reflections tell the story: sheet metal reflects environment — choose environment for emotional effect
- Motion blur implies power: specify whether frozen or in-motion based on intended energy
- Low camera angle + wide lens = dominance; high angle = landscape context
- Light should sculpt the body: side-raking light reveals curves and forms
- Environment is not neutral: desert = freedom, city = sophistication, mountain = adventure
- Chrome and glass are the detail story: extreme close-ups of badges, wheels, and headlights build desire

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Power Signaling:**
- Low Angle: Camera at or below axle height; vehicle looms, dominates, threatens
- Frontal Presence: Grill, headlights, badge centered and massive in frame
- Wheel Scale: Wheels fill 30–40% of frame; rubber, rim, brake caliper as detail obsession
- Motion Blur: Background streaks while vehicle stays sharp; speed made visible
- Dust/Spray: Dirt, water, snow kicking up from tires; force made tangible
- Engine Detail: Hood lines, vents, exhaust, carbon fiber = mechanical muscle
- Sound Visual: Exhaust pulse, vibration, window shake implied through motion

**Precision & Engineering:**
- Macro Detail: Paint depth, panel gaps, headlight internals, stitching
- Light Raking: Side light across body panels revealing curvature and reflection
- Reflection Quality: Environment mirrored in paint must be dramatic (sky, city, landscape)
- Color: Paint as emotional signal — red (passion), black (power), silver (tech), white (purity)
- Assembly: Robotic precision in manufacturing; hand-finishing for luxury
- Materials: Carbon fiber weave, aluminum machining, leather grain, wood veneer

**Aspiration & Environment:**
- Landscape Dominance: Vehicle in extreme environment — salt flats, mountains, coast, desert
- Architectural Context: Vehicle against modern architecture, bridge, tunnel, city skyline
- Time of Day: Golden hour or blue hour; light raking across body lines
- Weather: Storm, fog, rain, snow — vehicle as shelter and conqueror
- Negative Space: Vast landscape with small vehicle = adventure, freedom, exploration
- Human Scale: Driver visible but secondary; vehicle is the hero

**Environmental Drama:**
- Camera Movement: Tracking at speed, drone orbit, crane reveal, dolly parallel
- Lens: Wide for environmental scope; telephoto for compression and speed
- Shutter: Slow shutter (1/60–1/125) for motion blur; fast (1/1000+) for frozen power
- VFX: Lens flare, chromatic aberration, light streaks for speed sensation
- Atmosphere: Smoke from burnout, dust from drift, spray from water crossing

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Hero Reveal**
- Setup: Vehicle hidden by darkness, fabric, or architecture
- Sequence: Covering element removes; light sweeps across body
- Camera: Slow push-in or crane rise as vehicle is revealed
- Light: Dramatic single source; or sunrise breaking over horizon
- Emotional: Anticipation, desire, event, unveiling
- Best For: New model launch, concept reveal, auto show

**2. The Landscape Conqueror**
- Setup: Vehicle in extreme environment at speed
- Camera: Drone tracking, helicopter, or low tracking vehicle
- Environment: Salt flats, mountain pass, coastal highway, desert dune
- Motion: Full speed; dust/water/spray trailing
- Scale: Vehicle small against vast landscape = freedom, adventure
- Emotional: Escape, capability, frontier, mastery
- Best For: SUVs, trucks, adventure vehicles, off-road brands

**3. The Night City Predator**
- Setup: Vehicle moving through urban night environment
- Light: Streetlights, neon, headlights, taillight streaks
- Camera: Low tracking, car-to-car, or static with speed blur
- Reflection: Wet pavement reflecting lights; vehicle as glowing object
- Motion: Smooth, powerful, unstoppable urban flow
- Emotional: Power, nightlife, sophistication, danger
- Best For: Sports cars, sedans, luxury vehicles, EVs

**4. The Engineering Autopsy**
- Setup: Macro and detail shots of vehicle components
- Camera: Probe lens, macro, or dramatic close-up
- Subject: Engine bay, carbon fiber, stitching, dashboard, wheel
- Light: Raking, spot, or dramatic side light revealing texture
- Movement: Slow, deliberate; or assembly sequence
- Emotional: Craft, obsession, precision, mechanical beauty
- Best For: Luxury, supercar, manufacturing, heritage brands

**5. The Tunnel Run**
- Setup: Vehicle entering or exiting tunnel at speed
- Light: Dark tunnel interior contrasting with bright exit; or headlights illuminating walls
- Camera: Static at tunnel mouth; or tracking from behind
- Sound Visual: Exhaust reverberation implied through vibration
- Motion: Acceleration out of darkness into light
- Emotional: Birth, emergence, power unleashed, transformation
- Best For: Sports cars, performance EVs, motorcycles, racing

**6. The Human-Machine Fusion**
- Setup: Driver and vehicle as single entity
- Framing: Hands on wheel, eyes in rearview, foot on pedal
- Light: Dashboard glow on face; or environmental light through windshield
- Motion: G-force effects on face, vibration, focused expression
- Emotional: Control, skill, symbiosis, becoming machine
- Best For: Racing, performance driving, luxury experience, motorsport
