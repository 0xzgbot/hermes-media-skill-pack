# SKILL: Sports & Performance
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Brand Voice & Style

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of the high-intensity visual language that translates physical exertion into emotional narrative. Sports cinematography must make the viewer feel the heartbeat, the burn, the breathlessness, and the triumph. It operates at the edge of human limitation — where sweat becomes glory, failure becomes character, and community becomes tribe. Intensity is the baseline; everything else is modulation.

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
sports, athlete, performance, training, competition, running, fitness, sweat, intensity, champion, sports photography, action shot, freeze frame, athletic body, game, court, field, stadium

### CORE RULES
- Intensity must be visible: sweat, muscle tension, effort lines, exertion expression
- Freeze motion at peak: catch the moment of maximum physical expression
- Low-angle hero shots transform athletes into larger-than-life figures
- Shallow depth isolates athlete from environment at intimate moments; wide angle shows scale at epic moments
- Sweat and imperfection are assets: authentic effort beats perfect studio look
- Environmental context tells the sport's story: court, field, stadium, street, gym
- Color grading should increase energy: slightly boosted contrast and saturation signal intensity

---

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Intensity Signaling:**
- Camera: Close, immersive, often handheld or body-mounted; viewer is in the action
- Frame Rate: High speed (120–1000fps) for impact, sweat, impact, expression
- Shutter: Fast shutter (1/1000+) to freeze peak action; or slow for motion blur
- Light: Hard, directional, often environmental; stadium lights, sun, gym fluorescents
- Contrast: High; deep shadows and bright highlights = drama and struggle
- Color: Saturated team colors, skin tones flushed, environment vivid
- Sound Visual: Implied — heavy breathing, footfall, impact, crowd roar

**Sweat & Physical Truth:**
- Macro: Sweat droplets, muscle fiber activation, vein engorgement, skin flush
- Texture: Wet skin, soaked fabric, chalk dust, turf burn, blood, tape
- Expression: Grimace, focus, exhaustion, euphoria — unfiltered face
- Body: In motion, under load, at limit; never posed or static
- Environment: Field, court, track, gym, ring, pool — evidence of battle
- Imperfection: Mud, grass stains, torn clothing, taped injuries = authenticity

**Achievement & Triumph:**
- Moment: The peak — goal, finish line, lift, landing, buzzer-beater
- Reaction: Immediate emotional release — scream, collapse, tears, embrace
- Symbol: Trophy, medal, record board, scoreboard, raised arms
- Light: Often backlit or dramatically lit for silhouette/mythic quality
- Speed: Real-time for impact; slow-motion for emotional expansion
- Isolation: Hero moment — subject alone in frame at peak

**Community & Tribe:**
- Group: Team huddle, celebration pile, training together, locker room
- Touch: High fives, hugs, shoulder bumps, handshakes, piggyback
- Uniform: Matching kit = identity, belonging, shared sacrifice
- Diversity: Team as mixed unit; sport as universal language
- Support: Coach, trainer, family, fans — ecosystem of belief
- Ritual: Pre-game routine, chant, prayer, warm-up = shared psychology

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Peak Moment**
- Setup: Single frame of maximum exertion or impact
- Camera: High speed, tight, immersive
- Action: Ball hitting glove, foot striking turf, fist connecting, body landing
- Detail: Frozen sweat, muscle compression, facial grimace, equipment flex
- Light: Hard, dramatic, often backlit or stadium spot
- Emotional: Power, limit, sacrifice, peak human capability
- Best For: Equipment, energy drinks, leagues, fitness brands

**2. The Exhaustion Portrait**
- Setup: Post-effort subject bent over, hands on knees, gasping
- Camera: Eye level or low angle; close on face
- Detail: Sweat dripping, chest heaving, eyes focused or distant
- Environment: Empty field, quiet gym, dawn track — solitude after effort
- Light: Natural or practical; no beauty treatment
- Emotional: Vulnerability, resilience, humanity, earned rest
- Best For: Fitness apps, endurance brands, mental health, motivation

**3. The Team Organism**
- Setup: Team moving as single unit — drill, huddle, celebration
- Camera: Wide or tracking; showing group dynamics
- Action: Synchronized movement, shared breath, collective voice
- Uniform: Kit as skin; identity through clothing
- Light: Even for team; or dramatic for individual within group
- Emotional: Belonging, sacrifice, shared purpose, tribe
- Best For: Team sports, apparel, leagues, community brands

**4. The Underdog Rise**
- Setup: Narrative of overcoming — training montage, early morning, repetition
- Camera: Varied — montage pacing, quick cuts, multiple angles
- Action: Failure, retry, failure, retry, incremental improvement
- Environment: Humble — backyard, basement, public court, empty gym
- Light: Dawn, dusk, harsh practicals; no glamour
- Emotional: Determination, grit, hope, inevitability
- Best For: Inspiration, footwear, apparel, beverages, documentaries

**5. The Arena Spectacle**
- Setup: Massive stadium or event space with athlete as center
- Camera: Wide establishing to tight action; scale contrast
- Crowd: Visible energy, color, movement, noise made visual
- Light: Stadium spots, jumbotron glow, pyrotechnics, sunset
- Action: Entrance, anthem, kickoff, tip-off, first pitch
- Emotional: Myth, spectacle, collective ritual, shared transcendence
- Best For: Leagues, events, broadcasting, major campaigns

**6. The Solo Ritual**
- Setup: Individual athlete in private preparation
- Camera: Intimate, close, respectful distance
- Action: Taping laces, visualization, breathing, stretching, prayer
- Environment: Locker room, empty court, dawn road, basement gym
- Light: Personal, often dim or single source
- Emotional: Focus, sacrifice, mental game, invisible work
- Best For: Premium brands, mental performance, documentaries, motivation
