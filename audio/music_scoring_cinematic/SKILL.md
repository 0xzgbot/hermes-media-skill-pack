### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**The 6-Layer Music Score:**
- **Orchestral Foundation:** Strings, brass, woodwinds, percussion — emotional breadth
- **Electronic Texture:** Synth pads, pulses, arpeggiators — modern tension
- **Rhythmic Drive:** Percussion, drums, clicks — pace and energy
- **Melodic Theme:** Lead instrument carrying primary motif — recognition
- **Ambient Bed:** Drones, reverb tails, room tone — space and sustain
- **Stinger/Punctuation:** Hits, swells, risers — emphasis and transition

**Emotion-to-Music Mapping:**

| Emotion | Key / Mode | Tempo | Instrumentation | Technique |
|---------|-----------|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Heroic / Triumphant** | Major, tonic | 120–140 BPM | Brass + orchestral percussion | Fanfare, soaring strings |
| **Tense / Suspenseful** | Minor, modal | 80–100 BPM | Low strings, synth pulses | Ostinato, dissonance |
| **Romantic / Tender** | Major, lush | 60–80 BPM | Solo piano, strings, harp | Legato, rubato |
| **Melancholic / Sad** | Minor, descending | 50–70 BPM | Solo cello, piano, oboe | Sparse, solo voice |
| **Energetic / Exciting** | Major / Mixolydian | 128–150 BPM | Full orchestra + electronic | Driving rhythm, stabs |
| **Mysterious / Wonder** | Modal, whole tone | 70–90 BPM | Glass harmonica, celesta, synth | Arpeggios, unresolved chords |
| **Dark / Menacing** | Minor, Phrygian | 60–80 BPM | Low brass, distorted synth | Drones, cluster chords |
| **Nostalgic / Wistful** | Major, flat seventh | 60–75 BPM | Acoustic guitar, piano, strings | Simple diatonic melody |

**Tempo-to-Visual-Pacing Correlation:**
- **60 BPM:** One beat per second — contemplative, deliberate, grief
- **90 BPM:** Walking pace — natural, conversational, documentary
- **120 BPM:** Two beats per second — driving, active, commercial
- **140+ BPM:** Urgent, intense, action, climax

**The Leitmotif System:**
- **Character Theme:** 4–8 note melodic cell associated with specific character
- **Transformation:** Same motif in major/minor mode shift as character arc progresses
- **Counterpoint:** Two themes played simultaneously during character conflict
- **Fragmentation:** Motif broken into pieces during moments of crisis
- **Resolution:** Full statement of theme at climax or conclusion

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. The Heroic Fanfare**
- Key: Major, tonic
- Tempo: 120–140 BPM
- Instruments: Brass section, timpani, soaring strings
- Structure: Bold opening statement → development → triumphant recapitulation
- Emotional: Victory, achievement, aspiration, leadership
- Best For: Brand anthems, product launches, sports, corporate triumph

**2. The Tension Ostinato**
- Key: Minor, modal
- Tempo: 80–100 BPM
- Instruments: Low cellos, synth pulses, sub-bass
- Structure: Repeating 2–4 bar figure, gradually adding dissonance
- Emotional: Anxiety, anticipation, unresolved threat
- Best For: Thrillers, tech reveals, countdown sequences, problem/solution arcs

**3. The Emotional Swell**
- Key: Major or minor, flexible
- Tempo: 60–80 BPM, rubato
- Instruments: String section, solo piano, ambient pad
- Structure: Single long tone → gradual layering → peak → decay
- Emotional: Wonder, heartbreak, revelation, transcendence
- Best For: Brand purpose films, testimonials, nature, human stories

**4. The Electronic Pulse**
- Key: Modal or atonal
- Tempo: 90–128 BPM
- Instruments: Synth bass, arpeggiators, glitch percussion
- Structure: 4/4 pulse with evolving texture, drop at climax
- Emotional: Modern, innovative, tech-forward, urgent
- Best For: Tech products, startups, AI, data, innovation

**5. The Minimalist Build**
- Key: Major or modal
- Tempo: 70–90 BPM
- Instruments: Single instrument (piano/marimba/guitar) gradually joined by others
- Structure: Simple repeating pattern, additive process ( Reich/Glass influence)
- Emotional: Hypnotic, process-oriented, intellectual, satisfying
- Best For: Explainer videos, process documentation, craft stories

### AUDIO-VISUAL SYNCHRONIZATION

| Visual Event | Musical Response | Timing |
|-------------|------------------|--------|
| Hard cut | Stinger / hit / chord stab | Frame-accurate |
| Slow push-in | Crescendo / swell | 2–4 bars before reveal |
| Character entrance | Leitmotif statement | On-screen appearance |
| Cut to black | Reverb tail / silence | Immediate |
| Text reveal | Single note / punctuation | Synchronized to text pop |
| Product rotation | Arpeggio / ascending line | Follows motion direction |
| Landscape reveal | Orchestral tutti / drone | Sustained through shot |
| Fast montage | Driving rhythm / percussion | Constant pulse |

### TECHNICAL NOTES FOR AI GENERATION
- Use specific musical terms: "ostinato", "legato", "staccato", "arpeggio", "tutti"
- Specify BPM for precise tempo mapping to visual cuts
- Name instruments explicitly for timbral clarity
- Reference composers for style anchors: "Hans Zimmer epic", "Johannsson melancholy", "Reich minimalist"
- For FLUX/LTX: music must be planned as post-production layer; describe intended audio in shot notes
- Use "leitmotif" for recurring character/brand themes
- Dynamic markings (pp, mp, mf, f, ff) communicate volume arcs
