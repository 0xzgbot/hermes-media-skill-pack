# SKILL: Overcast Diffusion
## Version: 1.0 | Hermes Agent Lighting System

---

### DESCRIPTION
Mastery of heavily diffused natural light from complete cloud cover. Often maligned as "flat," overcast light is actually ideal for color saturation, subtle texture, and even illumination. The sky becomes a giant softbox (effectively infinite size), eliminating harsh shadows and specular highlights. Critical for macro, woodland, product, and fine-art photography.

### TRIGGER KEYWORDS
overcast, cloudy, diffused light, flat light, grey sky, soft light, even lighting, shadowless, cloudy day, diffuse natural light, soft natural, overcast portrait, woodland light, macro light, cool daylight

### CORE RULES
- Sky becomes a 180° softbox: shadows are open, soft, and wrap completely around subjects
- Color temperature 6000K–7500K: cool, not warm — never add warmth unless intentionally graded
- No harsh specular highlights: surfaces render with maximum detail in midtones
- Color saturation increases without direct sun bleaching — ideal for rich color work
- Direction is subtle zenith bias: slight overhead quality, not fully omnidirectional
- Works beautifully for: macro, botanical, fashion in rich fabrics, food, portraiture needing skin evenness
- Avoid when: depth, drama, or strong shadow is desired — use motivated fill instead

### TECHNICAL PARAMETERS

**Cloud Cover Characteristics:**
- Light Source: Entire sky dome (180°+ effective source)
- Quality: Extremely soft, wrap-around, shadowless
- Color Temperature: 6000K–7500K (cool, blue-shifted)
- Intensity: 1–3 stops less than direct sun (varies by cloud density)
- Consistency: Highly stable, minimal change over hours
- Direction: Omnidirectional with slight zenith bias

**Flat Light Advantages:**
- Dynamic Range: Minimal, easily captured in single exposure
- Skin Tones: Even, no harsh blemish emphasis
- Color Saturation: Increased due to absence of specular highlights washing out color
- Texture: Subtle, visible without harsh shadow competition
- Reflection Control: No direct reflections, view through glass/water easier

**Shadow Behavior:**
- Depth: Minimal, open shadows (Zone IV–V)
- Edge: Extremely soft, imperceptible transition
- Direction: Diffuse, no single direction (subtle zenith-down)
- Fill: Natural fill everywhere from sky bounce
- Contrast: Low, typically 1.5:1 to 2:1 inherent

**Color Saturation Boost:**
- Mechanism: Diffused light eliminates surface specular reflections that desaturate
- Polarizer Effect: Can still use polarizing filter to cut remaining reflected light from wet surfaces
- Result: Deep, rich colors without post-processing push
- Best Subjects: Autumn foliage, flowers, saturated fabrics, painted surfaces

### ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

**1. Woodland Photography**
- Overcast eliminates high dynamic range problems in forest
- Even light allows detail in shadows and highlights
- Wet foliage benefits: saturated colors, no glare
- Best: Dense canopy, moss, ferns, forest floors

**2. Macro & Close-Up**
- Diffused light reveals fine detail without shadow obstruction
- Color accuracy for scientific/documentation purposes
- Even illumination across frame
- Best: Insects, flowers, textures, product detail

**3. Portrait (Soft Beauty)**
- No directional shadows means no "bad side"
- Skin appears smoother, blemishes less pronounced
- Eyes: Soft, even catchlights from sky (large, diffuse)
- Risk: Can lack dimension; mitigate with slight side positioning

**4. Product & Catalog**
- Color accuracy paramount
- No reflections on glossy surfaces
- Even illumination across product
- Best: Clothing, ceramics, food, electronics

**5. Atmospheric Mood**
- Fog + overcast = infinite softbox with depth layers
- Muted colors, low contrast, ethereal quality
- Emotional: Melancholy, contemplation, isolation
- Best: Landscapes, solitary figures, abandoned places
